Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about the M3ak digital identity platform.

How do I verify my Moroccan ID?

Verification is simple. Navigate to "Identity" in your account settings, then follow the guided steps to scan your CNIE and confirm your biometric data.

What happens if I lose my phone?

You can recover your account using your registered email address. From the log in page, select "Forgot?" and follow the reset steps to regain access.

Is my biometric data secure?

Yes. All biometric data is encrypted end-to-end and stored under Kingdom of Morocco data protection standards. It is never shared with third parties.
